F. Self Reflection
This assignment helped me understand that an adoption recommendation is a bounded management posture, not a simple yes-or-no verdict on a technology. The primary case supported a bounded experiment only because the hypothetical organization was assumed to have test isolation, review capacity, and some ability to reverse actions. The contrast case moved to defer when those conditions disappeared. Adding the explicit bias check showed me that novelty, fear of missing out, sunk cost, and excessive caution should be examined through observable evidence rather than assumed motives. I also learned that a proof of concept can answer whether a system works technically, while a proof of value requires evidence against the current process. My recommendation remains conditional until system-specific performance, controls, and customer outcomes are demonstrated.

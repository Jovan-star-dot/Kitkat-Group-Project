# Minimum diffusion analysis requirements

Define the technology, exact task, baseline, social system, geography, evidence period and horizon. Assess general technology, application and comparable peers separately; keep internal adoption separate. At the general and application levels explicitly assess innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority and laggards, plus relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability and observability. Unknown is a legitimate evidence status.

Name relevant change agents, opinion leaders, standards, ecosystem conditions and complementary assets with source or inference labels. Explain the strongest signal, decisive factor, contrary case, confidence and reversal evidence. Preserve original dates and scope. One survey reused across reports is one evidence base. Posture alone cannot change observed external diffusion. Output timing conditions to the Adoption Agent without adopting, scaling or authorizing experiments.

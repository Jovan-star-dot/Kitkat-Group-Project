# Borderline innovation and incumbent-business cases

Four AI-generated synthetic stress cases; all facts/numbers are hypothetical. This is analytical review by the same Codex assistant, not independent ChatGPT runs or empirical validation. These are separate boundary tests, not additional fixed-D1 contrast tests: some intentionally change the technology.

| Case | Defensible bounded judgment | Vague criterion or superficial claim exposed | Distinguishing evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

## Incumbent-business counter-case
Routine curation is stipulated to supply 60% of fee revenue but 15% of contribution margin. Advisory services supply most contribution margin. Low-cost self-service could reduce fees yet create advisory demand rather than destroy the agency. Revenue decline alone cannot establish existential exposure. Neither substitution nor complementarity is observed. Preserve both interpretations and test them.

## Revision made
The original incremental criterion included stable customer relationships/value capture, which could incorrectly relabel incremental technology when its application changes the business model. Revised it to use capability/outcome discontinuity at the level being assessed. Also clarified that invention may coexist with implementation, implementation alone does not establish significant improvement, benchmark claims need scope boundaries, and viability analysis needs margin/adaptation evidence.

## Self Reflection

This assignment taught me that a technology label does not establish either innovation type or disruption. My first analysis was vulnerable to treating agentic AI as autonomous simply because the case used that label. The revision forced me to examine the described behavior, distinguish a fixed workflow from dynamic tool selection, and separate proposed benefits from demonstrated outcomes. The contrast and ambiguous tests also showed me that the same technology can have different application and business-model implications while the general classification remains stable. I learned that a credible disruption judgment requires evidence about what the system actually does, what it changes in the organization, and what remains hypothetical.

# Team Handoff – Promethean Technology Classification Agent

This folder contains Raphael Napoleon Hernando's Lab 4 specialist agent for team synthesis.

## Specialist role
Classify an emerging technology as Incremental, Breakthrough, or Potentially Promethean while keeping broad technological significance separate from the local application and organization decision.

## Current judgment
- General ET: LLM-based autonomous AI agents are **Potentially Promethean**, qualified, with moderate confidence.
- Strongest competing classification: **Breakthrough**.
- Local 3PL application: freight-invoice reconciliation is a bounded process breakthrough; broad Promethean potential does not automatically justify autonomous payment approval.

## Integration rule
The Synthesize Agent must not turn long-range technological significance into automatic advocacy for near-term adoption. Preserve the three-level distinction: ET generally → ET for the intended application → ET for that application in the organization.

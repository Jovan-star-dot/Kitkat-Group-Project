# Preserved Weakness / Failure — v0

## Weak output
"Organizational AI adoption has reached 88%, so agentic AI is becoming mainstream and the mid-sized 3PL risks falling behind if it does not move quickly toward autonomous freight-invoice approval."

## Why this is unsatisfactory
- It substitutes **overall AI adoption** for agentic-AI adoption.
- It substitutes **agentic-AI adoption** for application-specific maturity.
- It ignores organization-size differences.
- It turns a broad market statistic into adoption advocacy.
- It ignores the strongest conventional alternative.
- It makes an autonomy recommendation outside the Landscape Agent's authority.

## Revision
Every consequential signal must be labeled by scope and source type, compared with the strongest incumbent alternative, and translated only into a bounded landscape/monitoring implication.

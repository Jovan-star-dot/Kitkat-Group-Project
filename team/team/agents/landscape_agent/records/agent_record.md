## C. Three-Level Finding
**ET generally:** Agentic AI is accelerating but uneven. Capability and interoperability are advancing quickly, while enterprise scaling and reliable production use remain heterogeneous. Broad AI adoption must not be treated as agent maturity.  
**ET for this application:** Logistics production analogues are growing, but freight-invoice-specific evidence is weaker. Strongest conventional alternative: deterministic document extraction + rules/tolerance matching + ERP/TMS integration + human exception review.  
**ET for this application in this organization:** For a mid-sized fast-follower, comparable logistics deployments, integration standards, reliability and fully loaded economics matter more than general AI popularity. Direct evidence does not justify treating autonomous payment authority as mainstream.  
**Bounded management implication:** Continue supervised evaluation and benchmark the agentic layer against the incumbent workflow; monitor comparable peer evidence, interoperability, reliability, economics and control/regulatory signals.  
**Confidence / uncertainty:** Moderate on broad landscape position; lower on application-specific maturity and economics because comparable peer evidence and internal baseline data are limited.  
**What evidence or condition would change the conclusion:** Sustained comparable 3PL deployments showing auditable performance superior to the incumbent; standardized controlled ERP/TMS integration; materially better reliability/economics; or significant regulatory/control changes.

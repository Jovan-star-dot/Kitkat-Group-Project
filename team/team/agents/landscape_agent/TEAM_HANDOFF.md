# TEAM HANDOFF — Lab 8 Landscape Agent

## Landscape conclusion
Agentic AI is accelerating but uneven. Enterprise agent scaling is growing, logistics production analogues are real, and interoperability standards are strengthening. However, broad AI adoption and adjacent logistics deployments do not establish freight-invoice-specific maturity or autonomous financial authority.

## Most decision-relevant signal
**Growing production use of agentic workflows in logistics with human validation.** DHL reports agentic shipment booking/data enrichment at meaningful scale, but this remains an analogue rather than proof of autonomous invoice-payment readiness.

## Strongest competing / conventional alternative
Deterministic document extraction + rules/tolerance matching + ERP/TMS integration + human exception review.

## Monitoring trigger
Reassess the current landscape position if multiple comparable 3PLs show sustained, auditable agentic reconciliation performance that beats the incumbent workflow on exception quality, cycle time, reviewer burden, error rate and fully loaded cost.

## Final MoE interface requirements
The final intake/orchestrator should preserve evidence date/as-of date; source type; signal scope; application and organization context; strongest conventional alternative; most decision-relevant signal; stale/hype-sensitive claim; monitoring trigger and refresh cadence; and confidence/abstention conditions.

## Integration cautions
- Do not turn overall AI adoption into agentic/application maturity.
- Do not let vendor/operator forecasts substitute for independent evidence.
- Do not translate ecosystem maturity into adoption approval.
- Preserve the boundary between Landscape, Adoption and Organizational Adoption specialists.
- Recheck current evidence before final team synthesis if the landscape has materially changed.

# Organizational intake requirements

Collect evidence for the application, organization, requested stage, consequence/exposure and horizon. Use explicit unknowns, source, observation date, scope and verification status for each material fact. These are analytical evidence requirements, not authorization to invent records or alter the frozen schema.

1. Scope/stage: technology/application reference, proposed authority, manual/simpler baseline, intended users, decision stage, affected parties, consequences and horizon.
2. Leadership/authority: sponsor commitment, accepted charter, actual access/launch/funding rights, approvals and escalation/stop authority. A title is insufficient.
3. People/workflow: demonstrated competence, reviewer/support coverage, current/reviewed procedures, handoffs, exceptions, escalation, fallback and operational baseline. A desired task boundary is not workflow evidence.
4. Systems/data: actual interfaces, approved access, usable permitted data, linkage/provenance/quality tests, logging, containment and recovery for the stage. Do not infer unusable data from missing documentation or readiness from engineer/cloud availability.
5. Resources/ownership: pilot and continuing funding/staff allocations, implementation/usage/review/correction/maintenance/contingency costs, current delegation/approval record, accepted business/service/support ownership and incident/exit duties.
6. Change/evidence: representative user participation, workload/incentive concerns, response/feedback route, evidence date/scope/source/status and explicit unknowns. Do not infer resistance motives or universal acceptance.

Map stakeholders to users_stakeholders, capabilities to capabilities_constraints, consequences to risk_consequence_context and structured evidence/stage detail as labeled text in additional_context. Existing organization, industry_sector, business_need, management_question and time_horizon fields retain their meanings. No new top-level input/output fields are required.

For each gap, capture priority and reason, required action/question, evidence status, owner status/basis, dependency, proposed timing if needed, closure evidence and affected stage. A critical unknown requires an answerable evidence request, not a failing score. A known blocker remains blocking even with positive strengths elsewhere. Preserve approved funding facts separately from unknown delegation details; no new spending/change/expansion without relevant authority evidence.

F. Self Reflection
This assignment clarified the difference between choosing an attractive application and being ready to operate it. My first primary response rated workflow readiness too generously because it treated a proposed task boundary and an invitation to discuss exceptions as evidence that current routines were understood. The revision made me request evidence about actual handoffs, responsibilities, exception procedures, and review status. The contrast cases showed that readiness can fall because of undocumented interfaces, missing controls, unassigned ownership, or uncommitted resources even when the technology and application remain unchanged. I learned to treat unknown information as unknown, ask for the evidence needed to rate readiness, and identify the accountable operating owner as a condition for sustaining the work.

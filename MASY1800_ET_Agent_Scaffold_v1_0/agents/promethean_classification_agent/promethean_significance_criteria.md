# Team criteria and verified course alignment

Authority: Andres Fortino, Mastering the Craft of Emerging Technology Adoption, assigned 2026 draft, Chapter 11, especially 11.2 and 11.7, pp. 143–145 and 149–151. The complete relevant Chapter 11 pages 142–151 were read on October 20, 2026. No textbook file is republished here.

| Step | Course rule retained | Team safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| System | Evaluate an assembled usable system, not an isolated component. | Separate existing category from unimplemented local proposal. |
| Barrier | Identify a defining constraint and distinguish reduction from dissolution. | Do not convert missing evidence into a positive lower label. |
| Suppression | Test restoration of the prior state versus disruption of systems rebuilt on the technology. | Do not equate vendor switching difficulty with civilizational dependence. |
| Tiers | Dissolution across two or more tiers supports candidacy. | Multiple sectors do not automatically prove distinct transformed tiers. |
| Criteria | After the dissolution gate, examine order-of-magnitude, widespread impact, paradigm shift, catalytic effect, democratizing effect and irreversibility. | Use individual evidence statuses, no invented weights/cutoff. If not reached, defer explicitly. |
| Mode | Distinguish prospective potential from retrospective confirmation. | Unconfirmed irreversibility alone cannot eliminate all prospective potential. |
| Calibration | The chapter treats generative AI as Potential Promethean in its worked example. | Attribute the author's calibration; do not inherit it for every agent or application. |
| Context | Explain technology, application and organization separately. | No immediate adoption advocacy follows from a high label. |

The book's framework and calibration are conceptual course evidence. ReAct, Anthropic and Stanford references in evidence_sources.json provide bounded technical/practitioner/benchmark evidence. They do not independently establish two-tier dissolution, irreversible infrastructure, or customer-service value for the common cases.

Status vocabulary and Indeterminate are team implementation choices. There is no team numeric scoring rule. Potentially Promethean is normalized to the assignment's terminology; the textbook says Potential Promethean. No generic conditional pathway can bypass the actual course gates.

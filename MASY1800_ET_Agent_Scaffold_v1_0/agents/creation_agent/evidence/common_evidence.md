# Common creation evidence bundle
Review date: October 6, 2026. Same sources for primary and contrast.
Verification scope: original paper abstracts and arXiv submission histories, not full
replication or systematic literature review. Cases are hypothetical specifications.

- E1 Vaswani et al. (2017), Attention Is All You Need.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762
  Abstract describes attention-based Transformer architecture and parallelization.
  Supports predecessor architecture, not the universal origin of agents.
- E2 Brown et al. (2020), Language Models are Few-Shot Learners.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14165
  Supports large-scale pretrained language-model few-shot capabilities.
  Does not establish autonomous business-task reliability.
- E3 Lewis et al. (2020), Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401
  Abstract identifies knowledge-access/update/provenance challenges and combines
  pretrained generation with retrieved non-parametric memory.
  Does not establish company-data accuracy or that all agents require RAG.
- E4 Yao et al. (first submitted 2022; ICLR 2023), ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629
  Supports interleaving reasoning traces and actions accessing external environments.
  Does not establish a sole inventor or unrestricted transaction authority.
- E5 Schick et al. (2023), Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761
  Supports learning API choice, call timing, arguments, and use of results.
  Does not prove reliable arbitrary API execution.

Interpretation rule: the synthesis connecting these contributions is an analytical
lineage interpretation. Broad commercial motives and the complete economic/social
history remain unsupported by this limited bundle. Conventional API/data plumbing
and state are architectural dependencies, not inventions attributed to these papers.
